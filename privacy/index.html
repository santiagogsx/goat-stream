Goat Stream privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026. Applies to Goat Stream for Windows, Xbox, Android TV, Fire TV, Samsung, LG, Roku and web browsers.

The short version

Goat Stream plays your own movies and shows from your own PC and your own cloud drives. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share your personal information, and we do not run a server that stores your data.

What stays on your devices

Your library, watch progress, settings, linked devices and cover art are stored on your PC and your TV or console. They are sent only between your own devices on your home network.

Cloud drives (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, you sign in with that company, and the access it gives is stored on your own device. It is used only to list and play your videos. A small sign-in helper may be used once to complete the sign-in; it does not keep your files or your sign-in. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and that company's own privacy policy applies to your account with them.

Services the app talks to

  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — movie and show titles are looked up to fetch covers and descriptions.
  • GitHub — the PC and GitHub versions check for new versions of the app.
  • Watch Together (optional) — when you start a watch party, a public connection service introduces the devices to each other; the video and sound then go directly between the people watching.
  • Streaming-app shortcuts — opening Netflix, a store page or another app is handled by that app or store.

Children

Goat Stream does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/santiagogsx/goat-stream/issues.
